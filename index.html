Aura

Aura is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Mac. It is a private project, used by its owner only, and it is not offered to anyone else.

This app's only use of Google is to read the owner's own Gmail (read-only), so that Aura can search his mail and remind him of replies he still owes. Aura never sends, deletes, moves or changes any mail.

Everything Aura reads stays on the owner's Mac. See the privacy policy.
